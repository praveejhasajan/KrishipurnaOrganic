Why Source Makhana from Krishipurna Organic?

As a leading makhana exporter from India, we understand the unique requirements of Canada importers. Our Fox Nuts is sourced directly from certified organic farms and processed under strict quality controls.

Product Specifications

  • Scientific Name: Euryale ferox
  • Available Grades: Lava, Murhari, Stuffed, Roasted, Puffs
  • Packaging: Bulk bags (10kg, 25kg), Retail packs (50g-500g), Custom private label
  • Shelf Life: 12-18 months
