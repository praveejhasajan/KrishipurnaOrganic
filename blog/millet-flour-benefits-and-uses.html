Millet Flour Benefits and Uses: Complete Guide 2026

Millet Flour: The Gluten-Free Super Flour

Millet flour is gaining popularity as a nutritious, gluten-free alternative to wheat flour. Made by grinding whole millets into fine powder, millet flour retains all the nutritional benefits of the grain.

Benefits of Millet Flour

  • Gluten-Free: Safe for people with celiac disease
  • High in Fiber: Supports digestive health
  • Rich in Minerals: Iron, calcium, magnesium, phosphorus
  • Low GI: Helps manage blood sugar
  • Versatile: Can be used in baking, cooking, and beverages

Uses of Millet Flour

  • Baking: Bread, cookies, muffins, pancakes
  • Traditional: Roti, dosa, idli, porridge
  • Beverages: Smoothies, health drinks
  • Industrial: Baby food, snack manufacturing
