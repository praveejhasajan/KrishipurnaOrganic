15 Amazing Health Benefits of Makhana (Fox Nuts) - Complete Guide 2026

Why Makhana is Called a Superfood

Makhana, also known as Fox Nuts or Euryale Ferox, has been a staple in Indian and Chinese traditional medicine for centuries. These small, white, fluffy seeds from the lotus plant are now gaining global recognition as a superfood.

1. Rich in Antioxidants

Makhana contains powerful antioxidants including flavonoids and phenolic compounds that help fight free radicals and reduce oxidative stress.

2. Supports Heart Health

The low sodium and high potassium content in makhana makes it excellent for heart health, helping regulate blood pressure and supporting healthy heart rhythm.

3. Aids Weight Loss

With only 350 calories per 100 grams and high fiber content, makhana is an ideal snack for weight management.

4. Regulates Blood Sugar

Makhana has a low glycemic index, making it suitable for people with diabetes. Research supports makhana's anti-diabetic properties.

5. Improves Kidney Function

In Ayurvedic medicine, makhana has been traditionally used to support kidney health with its natural astringent properties.

6. Anti-Aging Properties

The antioxidants in makhana help combat signs of aging by protecting skin cells from damage.

7. Supports Digestive Health

The high fiber content promotes healthy digestion and acts as a prebiotic for beneficial gut bacteria.

8. Strengthens Bones

Makhana is a good source of calcium and magnesium, essential for maintaining bone density.

9. Reduces Inflammation

Anti-inflammatory compounds in makhana help reduce chronic inflammation linked to numerous diseases.

10. Improves Cognitive Function

B vitamins and amino acids in makhana support brain health, memory, and concentration.

11. Natural Sleep Aid

Makhana contains natural sedative compounds that can help improve sleep quality.

12. Supports Male Fertility

Zinc content and antioxidant properties support healthy sperm production.

13. Detoxifies the Body

Natural compounds in makhana support the body's detoxification processes.

14. Manages Blood Pressure

The combination of low sodium and high potassium helps regulate blood pressure naturally.

15. Rich in Protein

Makhana contains approximately 9-10 grams of protein per 100 grams, excellent for vegetarians.
