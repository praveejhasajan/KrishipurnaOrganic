Makhana Recipes

10 Easy Makhana Recipes for Beginners - From Snacks to Main Course

Discover the Versatility of Makhana in Your Kitchen

Makhana is one of the most versatile ingredients in Indian cuisine. From crispy snacks to rich curries, these nutritious seeds can be transformed into dozens of delicious dishes.

1. Classic Roasted Makhana

Dry roast 2 cups makhana on medium heat for 8-10 minutes until crispy. Add 1 tsp ghee, salt, and a pinch of turmeric. Perfect guilt-free snack!

2. Makhana Curry (Makhana Kofta)

Soak makhana for 30 minutes. Make koftas by mixing with mashed potato and spices. Fry until golden. Prepare onion-tomato gravy and add koftas. Simmer for 15 minutes.

3. Makhana Biryani

Layer parboiled rice with roasted makhana and biryani masala. Add saffron milk and cook on dum for 20 minutes.

4. Makhana Kheer (Pudding)

Soak and grind 1 cup makhana. Boil 1 liter milk, add makhana paste, sugar, and cardamom. Garnish with nuts.

5. Makhana Tikki (Cutlets)

Grind 2 cups roasted makhana. Mix with mashed potato, green chilies, and spices. Shape into tikki and shallow fry.

6. Makhana Raita

Whisk 1 cup yogurt with 1 cup roasted makhana, cumin powder, and salt. Refrigerate and serve chilled.

7. Makhana Chaat

Mix 2 cups roasted makhana with chopped onions, tomatoes, and green chutney. Sprinkle chaat masala.

8. Makhana Paratha

Mix 1 cup makhana flour with wheat flour and spices. Roll into parathas and cook on tawa with ghee.

9. Makhana Energy Balls

Grind 1 cup roasted makhana with dates, nuts, and coconut. Roll into balls. Perfect pre-workout snack!

10. Makhana Soup

Boil 1 cup makhana with vegetables in stock. Blend until smooth. Add cream and season to taste.
