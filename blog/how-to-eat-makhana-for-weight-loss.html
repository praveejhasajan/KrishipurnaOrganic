How to Eat Makhana for Weight Loss: Complete Guide 2026

Makhana: The Ultimate Weight Loss Snack

If you're looking for a healthy, satisfying snack that supports your weight loss goals, makhana (fox nuts) might be the perfect choice. These low-calorie, nutrient-dense seeds are now recognized as a superfood for weight management.

Why Makhana is Perfect for Weight Loss

  • Low in Calories: Only 350 calories per 100 grams roasted makhana
  • High in Fiber: Keeps you feeling full for longer periods
  • Low Glycemic Index: Helps maintain stable blood sugar levels
  • Plant-Based Protein: 9-10g protein per 100g supports muscle maintenance
  • Nutrient-Dense: Rich in magnesium, potassium, phosphorus, and B vitamins

Best Ways to Eat Makhana for Weight Loss

1. Roasted Makhana (35-40 calories per cup)

Dry roast makhana in a pan until crispy. Season with a pinch of salt, pepper, or chaat masala.

2. Makhana Trail Mix

Combine roasted makhana with nuts, seeds, and dried fruits for a satisfying trail mix.

3. Makhana Khichdi

Light, protein-rich khichdi made with makhana and moong dal. Ideal for a filling dinner.

4. Makhana Porridge

Grind makhana into powder and cook with low-fat milk for a nutritious breakfast.

5. Makhana Raita

Add roasted makhana to yogurt for a cooling, protein-rich side dish.

When to Eat Makhana for Maximum Weight Loss

  • Morning: Roasted makhana as a mid-morning snack (10-11 AM)
  • Evening: Replace unhealthy evening snacks (4-5 PM)
  • Night: Light makhana porridge as a light dinner option

Recommended Daily Intake

For weight loss, consume 1-2 cups (30-60 grams) of roasted makhana per day.
